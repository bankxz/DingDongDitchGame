# Arena Lobby Asset Kit — Build Notes

All geometry is generated by Python scripts running headless Blender 5.0.1 (the `bpy` module).
Every image in `renders/` is an actual Cycles render of these models. None of them is concept art.

## Layout

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `scripts/lobbylib.py` | Shared helpers: bevelled primitives, sweeps, text, shared material palette, render rig, contact sheets |
| `scripts/build_0N_*.py` | One script per asset. Builds it, saves `blend/0N_*.blend`, renders `renders/<asset>_*.png` |
| `scripts/make_textures.py` | Tileable cobblestone texture: `textures/cobblestone_albedo.png` + `_normal.png`, 6 × 6 studs per repeat |
| `scripts/export_roblox.py` | Writes `fbx/*.fbx` (one model per asset or prop), `fbx/roblox_manifest.json` and `src/shared/LobbyAssetStyler.luau` |
| `blend/` | Blender files, one collection tree per asset (`ASSET_0N_*` → sub-collections) |
| `renders/` | Inspection renders (front/right/rear/left ortho, 3/4, details, reference-angle matches) and contact sheets |

Conventions: 1 Blender unit = 1 stud, +Z up, model fronts face −Y. A Roblox character is about 5 studs tall.
Trees, grass, bushes and flowers are deliberately **not** modelled. Planters are empty and have a soil top.

## Rebuild

```bash
python -m venv … && pip install bpy pillow   # bpy needs Python 3.11
python scripts/build_01_arch.py              # add --no-render to skip renders
python scripts/build_06_props.py
python scripts/export_roblox.py
```

## Reference analysis (Phase 0)

Sources: 5 lobby images (main view, A–D sheet, wide plaza view, front/aerial/side/rear sheet, floating castle), plus the dedicated sheets for the arch, pedestals, market shop, craft shop and arena facade.

Priority used: dedicated prop sheet > close-ups > lobby screenshots (style and scale) > aerial (placement) > inference.

### Inconsistencies found and how each was resolved

1. **Arch sheet, left vs right view.** The right view is not a true mirror. The banner overhangs the column's right edge in both views, and the far railing post is missing on one side. *Resolution:* module A follows the left view. The mirrored module is produced by mirroring about the crown plane x = 15.
2. **Arch sheet, middle view.** Its perspective is impossible: the floor slab recedes toward the camera while the arch recedes away from it. *Resolution:* used only for depth and thickness (square column, arch depth ≈ 0.75 × column width, banner standing off the column face).
3. **Railing material.** The arch sheet shows a **bronze** baluster railing; the lobby screenshots show **white marble** balusters with gold bands. *Resolution:* the arch module keeps bronze (its own sheet is the authority). The perimeter balustrade kit (asset 06) is marble, because the lobby images are the only reference for it.
4. **Arch orientation.** In the lobby the arches span the walkway, while on the sheet the railing runs parallel to the arch plane. *Resolution:* the sheet is followed. When placed in the lobby, the module is rotated so the arch crosses the road.
5. **Pedestal count and colours** vary between lobby shots (5–8 glyphs per side, with order and colours changing). *Resolution:* the 5 glyphs on the dedicated sheet are authoritative. Extra glyphs were added only where the lobby images show them clearly.
6. **Arena gate.** The lobby images show a full castle with tall towers behind the gate; the facade sheet shows a single wall-and-tower facade. *Resolution:* the facade sheet is followed. The castle backdrop is out of scope.

## Roblox pipeline

`export_roblox.py` works like this:
- Applies all modifiers and merges each model **per material**, so you get one MeshPart per material, named `<Model>__<Material>`.
- Splits any MeshPart above 18k triangles.
- Writes box-projected UVs at 8 studs per tile, so Roblox surface materials tile evenly.
- Exports FBX with the origin at the floor centre.

After importing with the 3D Importer, run:

```lua
require(game.ReplicatedStorage.Shared.LobbyAssetStyler).styleAll(workspace)
```

Textured cobble surfaces: upload `textures/cobblestone_albedo.png` and `cobblestone_normal.png` to Roblox and paste the two `rbxassetid://` ids into the `TEXTURES` table at the top of `LobbyAssetStyler.luau`. The styler then adds a SurfaceAppearance to every cobble part. Until then those parts fall back to a plain blue-grey Slate.

The styler sets Material, Color, Reflectance and Transparency: Marble → `Marble`, emissive → `Neon` with CastShadow off, wood → `Wood`, cobble → `Slate`, cloth → `Fabric`, gold → reflective SmoothPlastic. It then rescales each model to its Blender size in studs, so the result doesn't depend on how the importer converts units.

**Not yet verified in Studio.** There is no Roblox Studio in this cloud environment. Import, scale and appearance must be checked on a machine with Studio.

Blender-only shading (procedural marble veins, wood grain, stripe patterns, emission bloom) does not carry over to Roblox. There it is approximated by Roblox materials and Neon. Baking these to SurfaceAppearance textures is the next step if closer texture fidelity is needed.

## Lobby map assembly (Blender)

`scripts/lobby_layout.py` builds `blend/lobby_assembled.blend`. Every finished asset is pulled from its own `.blend` as a collection instance, so edits to an asset carry through to the map when the script is re-run. It renders `renders/lobby_*.png`, collected in `lobby_assembly_sheet.png`.

**Layout,** taken from the spawn-view, aerial and plaza references:
- The spawn plaza is at the south. A curved balustrade sits on its curb.
- Five cobble road tiles run north to the arena gate.
- Five mirrored arch pairs span the road every 16 studs, and the EVENTS banner hangs from the first pair.
- Nine ability pedestals stand between the arch columns, angled toward the spawn.
- The market and craft shops sit on an east terrace. A west overlook terrace holds planters and lanterns.
- Lantern posts flank both ends of the road.
- The floating island has a marble deck, a gold edge band and a stepped underside.
- The balustrade runs around the whole perimeter. Every straight edge is 12 + 10n studs long, so the modules fit with no gaps or overlaps. The one exception is the plaza shoulder module, stretched 9%.
- Box figures 5 studs tall stand in several areas as player-scale references.

**Choices that depart from the references:**
- The road is 5 arch bays long; the spawn reference shows roughly that many before the gate.
- The pedestals fill 3 bays per side rather than running all the way to the gate, because there are only 9 glyph variants.
- The west terrace has no counterpart in the references. It exists so the balustrade module grid closes on both sides, and it now holds the two mode portals.

The same layout is written to `src/shared/LobbyLayout.luau` for a future Studio assembly. No Studio assembler has been written yet.

## Correction: crisp edges, flat masonry (after your feedback)

You pointed out that everything looked like rounded, bevelled squares, which the references don't show. Two root causes, both fixed at the source:

1. **`lobbylib.add_bevel` rounded every edge** with a wide two-segment bevel, giving pillowy blocks. Every bevel request is now scaled to 35% (`BEVEL_SCALE`), and small ones become single-segment chamfers. Forms stay crisp and planar with only the thin highlight edge visible on the sheets.
2. **The arena gate's stonework (`blocks()`) was built from loose rounded cubes**, each pushed in or out at random, with open gaps. It is now flush, crisp blocks over a darker mortar core in the same mesh, so walls read as flat surfaces with thin joint seams, as on the facade sheet.

The mode portals' pillars and lintel were also block stacks; they are now smooth marble shafts and a smooth beam, matching the gate's inner pillars on the sheet. All assets, the lobby renders and the FBX exports were regenerated.

## Castle revision (after your feedback)

- **Bricks are a texture, not geometry.** Castle walls, towers, keep, gatehouse face and platform sides are now solid surfaces using `textures/castle_brick_albedo.png` + `_normal.png`. The texture is generated by `make_textures.py`: white-lavender ashlar, 4.0 × 3.0-stud blocks in running bond, light seams and faint veins on some blocks, 12 studs per repeat, seamless.
- **Seamless UVs:** they are projected from world coordinates after every piece is placed, so the pattern continues across separate walls and towers.
- **Gate face:** a textured slab with the arch cut out, rather than a field of modelled blocks.
- **Underside:** the boulders are fused into one solid rock mass (`FC_IslandUnderside`). They are unioned with a voxel remesh, then reduced to about 4.6k faceted faces with flat shading.
- **Size:** about 31.7k triangles, footprint about 158 × 158 studs.
- **In Roblox:** upload the two castle_brick PNGs and paste their asset IDs into the `castle_brick` entry of `TEXTURES` in `LobbyAssetStyler.luau`. Until then the walls fall back to plain Slate.
